Performance

Unload idle tabs after
Background tabs you have not looked at free their memory and reload when you click them. Tabs playing audio are never unloaded.
Low-memory mode (experimental)
Shares processes between tabs and turns on Chromium's low-end mode. Uses less memory but may feel less smooth. Restart the browser after changing it.
Freeze background tabs (experimental)
After 30 seconds out of view, a tab keeps its place but stops running scripts, which saves CPU and battery. Tabs playing audio are never frozen. Pages that rely on live connections may reconnect when you return.
Spell check
Underlines misspelled words in text boxes. Turning it off saves a little memory. Applies to tabs opened afterwards.

Passwords, autofill and history

Offer to save passwords
Saved passwords are encrypted with your computer's key store (macOS Keychain, Windows data protection).
Autofill saved passwords
Shows your saved logins under a login box. Nothing is filled until you click one.
Autofill saved addresses and info
Fills name, email, phone and address when you click a matching form field.

Terminal

Start with opencode
Opens opencode when the terminal starts, and offers to install it if it is missing. Quit opencode to get a normal shell. Takes effect the next time the terminal starts (press Restart in it).
