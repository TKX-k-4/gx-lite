Performance

Unload idle tabs after
Background tabs you have not looked at free their memory and reload when you click them. Tabs playing audio are never unloaded.
Low-memory mode (experimental)
Shares processes between tabs and turns on Chromium's low-end mode. Uses less memory but may feel less smooth. Restart the browser after changing it.

Terminal

Start with opencode
Opens opencode when the terminal starts, and offers to install it if it is missing. Quit opencode to get a normal shell. Takes effect the next time the terminal starts (press Restart in it).
